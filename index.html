Tolga Demiryol

Professor of Political Science · Dean, Faculty of Economics, Administrative and Social Sciences, Altınbaş University, Istanbul

I study how economic interdependence becomes an instrument of power: energy, corridors, sanctions and defence production, with a focus on middle powers such as Türkiye and Canada and their relations with China, Russia and the West.

About

I hold a PhD in Political Science from the University of Virginia and was a Fung Global Fellow at Princeton University's Institute for International and Regional Studies (2018–19). Before joining Altınbaş, I taught at the University of New Haven and Quinnipiac University. At Altınbaş I founded and directed the Energy and Environment Research Center (2019–23), chaired the Department of Political Science and International Relations, and now serve as Dean.

I have served as an expert evaluator for Horizon 2020 and Horizon Europe and edit the Aurum Journal of Social Sciences. My work has appeared in Third World Quarterly, Turkish Studies, Higher Education and the Georgetown Journal of International Affairs, and in policy outlets including the Atlantic Council, FPRI, the Heinrich Böll Foundation, the CDA Institute, NAADSN and the Canadian Global Affairs Institute.
